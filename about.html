Hi there, this is Humphrey.

I was an undergraduate student at the Australian National University studying Data Analytics and Politics, Philosophy and Economics. I am currently a research assistant at QuantEcon, ANU Research School of Finance, Actuarial Studies and Statistics (RSFAS), and Data61, CSIRO. My research interest lies in statistical learning, high-performance computing, privacy-preserving machine learning, and data-driven computational social science.

When I have free time, I enjoy hiking around Canberra and traveling to taste different food. If I were to rank the cities I've visited, my top three would be Kunming (where I was born and raised), Kyoto (known for its good food and beautiful sunsets), and Canberra (where I currently reside).

Aside from that, I spend most of my time working with data and creating compelling visualizations that illuminate the stories behind the statistics. I like modeling data (and I can list out a handful of models to a specific question in hand!), especially when I allowed to be creative.
