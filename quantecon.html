QuantEcon

QuantEcon is a nonprofit organization dedicated to improving economic modeling by enhancing computational tools for economists. We use cutting-edge technology to create open-source software and open-access educational resources for economics.

For me, QuantEcon has been the place where I am supported by kind and smart friends and professors to demonstrate deep concepts. Here is a list of QuantEcon lectures and economic workshops to which I have made significant contributions under the supervision of Professor John Stachurski and Professor Thomas J. Sargent.
